Glimerz is an editorial site focused on practical home decor and kitchen inspiration. Our goal is to help readers make informed decisions and find ideas they can realistically use in their own homes.

## How We Publish

Every article is reviewed before publication. We use research and, where appropriate, AI-assisted tools to help with drafting, organization, or research, but automated tools do not independently decide what is published. A human editor reviews the final article for usefulness, accuracy, clarity, originality, and relevance to Glimerz readers.

## What We Look For

We prioritize practical explanations, original observations, useful comparisons, styling ideas, organization advice, and actionable guidance. Articles should provide value even when a reader does not purchase anything we recommend.

## Product Recommendations

When we recommend a product, it is presented as an optional resource rather than the purpose of the article. Product information such as price, availability, specifications, and retailer policies can change, so readers should confirm current details on the retailer's website before purchasing.

Some product links are affiliate links. Glimerz may earn a commission from qualifying purchases at no additional cost to the reader. Affiliate relationships do not determine our editorial conclusions.

## Sponsored and Guest Content

Sponsored or guest content is reviewed before publication and is clearly identified where applicable. We do not accept irrelevant, deceptive, copied, or low-quality submissions.

## Updates and Corrections

We may update articles when information becomes outdated or when a factual error is identified. If you notice an error or have useful information to add, please contact us at [help.glimerz@gmail.com](mailto:help.glimerz@gmail.com).

## Our Standard

Glimerz is intended to be useful first and commercial second. We want readers to leave with an idea, explanation, or practical solution they can use, whether or not they click a product link.
