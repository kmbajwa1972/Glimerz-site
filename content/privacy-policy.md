This Privacy Policy explains how Glimerz ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit glimerz.com (the "Site") or use our social media publishing integration ("the App").

## Information We Collect

We do not require you to create an account or submit personal information to read our content. If you contact us directly (for example, by email), we may collect your name, email address, and the contents of your message.

## Social Media Account Connections (Facebook, Instagram, Pinterest)

Glimerz offers an integration ("the App") that allows a business to connect their own Facebook Page, Instagram Business account, and/or Pinterest account so that content can be published to those accounts on their behalf.

**What we access.** When you connect an account through the App, we request access only to the information needed to publish content on your behalf, which may include:

- Your Page or profile name and basic account information
- The list of Pages, boards, or Instagram Business accounts you manage, so you can choose where to publish
- The ability to create posts, pins, or media on the account(s) you explicitly connect

We do not access your personal messages, contacts, or any data unrelated to publishing content to the account(s) you connect.

**How we use this access.** Access is used exclusively to carry out actions you or your authorized AI agent request — for example, publishing a post, pin, or image to an account you've connected. We do not use this access to read, analyze, or repurpose your content for any other business or advertising purpose.

**How we store this access.** When you connect an account, we store an access token (a credential issued by Facebook, Instagram, or Pinterest) that lets the App act on your behalf until you disconnect. We do not store your account password — authentication happens directly with Facebook, Instagram, or Pinterest through their own login process. Stored tokens are used solely to maintain your connection and are not shared, sold, or disclosed to any third party.

**Your control.** You may disconnect any connected account at any time, which revokes the App's access. You can do this either by contacting us at the email below, or directly through the account settings of the platform you connected (Facebook, Instagram, or Pinterest each let you revoke third-party app access from their own settings).

**Data retention.** Stored tokens are retained only for as long as your account remains connected, or until you request disconnection, whichever comes first.

Information accessed through connected social media accounts is used solely as described above, to publish content on your behalf.
