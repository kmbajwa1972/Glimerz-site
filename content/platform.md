Glimerz Social Publishing is a service operated by Glimerz that lets businesses connect their own Facebook Page and Instagram accounts to automate their social media publishing.

After a business authorizes access through Facebook Login, Glimerz Social Publishing uses that access only to publish and manage content the business has created, on their behalf and under their control. We do not use a client's data for any purpose beyond providing this publishing service to them, and clients can revoke access at any time through their own Facebook account settings.
